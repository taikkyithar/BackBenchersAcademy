# Content licensing / အကြောင်းအရာ လိုင်စင်

This repository holds three different kinds of material. They are licensed differently.

## 1. Code (crawler, web app, build scripts) — MIT

Everything under `crawler/`, `web/`, `scripts/` and the build files: [MIT License](LICENSE).

## 2. Original learning content we create — CC BY 4.0

Lesson scripts, 3D scenes, interactive exercises, explainer videos, translations and metadata created by
BackBenchers Academy contributors (everything under `content/` and `catalog/` that is not a copy of a textbook)
are released under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Attribute as "BackBenchers Academy contributors".

## 3. Ministry of Education textbooks — NOT ours, NOT relicensed

The KG–Grade 12 textbooks, teacher guides and syllabi indexed in `catalog/` are the work of the
**Ministry of Education, Republic of the Union of Myanmar** (Basic Education Curriculum and Textbook
Committee, with development partners such as JICA for the primary "CREATE" curriculum) and, for some
recent Grade 10–12 editions, the **NUG Ministry of Education**. Copyright stays with the respective publishers.

* This repository does **not** contain the PDF files. It contains a *catalog* (public URLs, hashes, sizes,
  page counts) and a crawler that fetches them from where they are already publicly distributed.
* The books were released to the public free of charge for education (MoE distributed them via its own
  MDEP "DBE Box" site and Google Drive during 2020, and the Department of Myanmar Examinations still hosts
  KG guides). We mirror them only for **non-commercial educational use** so every child in Myanmar can learn
  from the national curriculum for free. The same "listed for reference, subject to copyright" notice used by
  existing community libraries (edu4mm.com) applies here.
* Derived assets we generate from the textbooks (extracted text, page images used inside lessons) stay under
  the textbook copyright and the same non-commercial educational use.

## Takedown / ဖယ်ရှားပေးရန် တောင်းဆိုခြင်း

If you are a rights holder and want a file removed from the catalog or from any mirror we operate, open an issue
titled `takedown: <catalog id>` or email the maintainers. We remove promptly and without argument.
